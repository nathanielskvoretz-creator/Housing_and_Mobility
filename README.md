This is a personal project exploring a cursory look at the relationship between housing and population. My end goal is to examine the relationship between housing availability and economic mobility. My intial hypothesis is that greater housing avalability induces greater economic mobility. Mechanistically, workers who can more readily access larger labor markets gives them a greater advantage in finding better employment opportunities and thus greater economic mobility; i.e. given two cities A and B, which both have relatively equal emploment opportunties, if city B has greater housing avalability, we would expect city B to have greater economic mobility.
